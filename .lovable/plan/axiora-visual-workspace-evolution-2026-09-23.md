# Axiora — Visual Workspace Evolution

## Goal
Transform Home from a stack of expanded AI result cards into a calm visual map of meaningful work. Keep the central “What are you trying to accomplish?” input as the anchor and preserve Axiora’s existing intelligence, data, evidence, and Decision Briefing behavior exactly.

## Changes

1. **Compact work nodes**
   - Replace persistent full-response cards with compact nodes showing title, attention/state, workstream when useful, and one short next-action or status line.
   - Give decisions, clarification-needed items, blockers, active work, later work, and completed work distinct but restrained treatments.
   - Remove the predetermined polygon styling; shape and emphasis will come from content and state.

2. **Progressive detail**
   - Ordinary work nodes open an accessible detail panel containing Axiora’s interpretation, proposed steps, next action, uncertainty, and classification correction.
   - Clarification nodes open directly to the existing answer interaction.
   - Dependency nodes expose the existing possible-blocker confirmation or rejection controls.
   - Decision nodes continue to open the existing Decision Briefing directly.

3. **Organic spatial organization**
   - Arrange work by existing inferred workstreams without Projects, empty groups, columns, or a rigid hierarchy.
   - Use responsive placement, scale, spacing, and visual weight to reflect the stored state only: attention work is prominent, Next is quieter, Later and Completed recede, and Blocked relationships are visible when known.
   - Keep sparse workspaces spacious and allow related work to feel grouped without generating extra work items.

4. **Responsive behavior**
   - Desktop uses a varied spatial composition rather than a uniform card grid.
   - Mobile becomes a clear, touch-friendly sequence preserving the same hierarchy instead of shrinking the desktop canvas.
   - The central input remains visually dominant on both.

## Protected behavior
No changes to the four modes, AI interpretation, timing rules, clarification rules, inferred-step language, dependency logic, persistence, Decision Briefing, evidence flow, or decision data model.

## Verification
- Confirm desktop and mobile layout, sparse and populated workspace states, and reload behavior.
- Confirm compact nodes and detail disclosure for Simple, Ambiguous, and Dependency work.
- Confirm decisions remain distinct and route to the existing Briefing.
- Confirm clarification, blocker responses, and classification correction still persist.
- Confirm the final preview builds without errors.
